# Teacher Product MVP — September 2026

Goal by 30 September: working universal MVP, 3–5 pilot teachers, at least one teacher ready to pay.

## Phase 1 — started 14 Sep
- Separate Railway service: `teacher-admin-pilot`
- Universal teacher onboarding
- Teacher profile: name, subject, format, student count
- Teacher cabinet
- Students: add, list, archive

## Phase 2
- Weekly schedule
- Lesson creation
- One-click reschedule
- Student notifications about changes

## Phase 3
- Homework status
- Payment tracking
- Simple student status

## Pilot
- Pilot teacher #1: inbound lead who asked to purchase the product
- Target: 3–5 real teachers
- Measure: real student data entered, schedule used, at least one reschedule/reminder completed, willingness to continue and pay
